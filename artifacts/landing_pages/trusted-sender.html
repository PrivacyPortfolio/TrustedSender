What IS a TrustedSender?

A TrustedSender is an identifiable person, or an agent authorized to represent an identifiable person, who may or may not be afilliated with an organization.
A TrustedSender consents by written agreement, to RECEIVE email messages from PrivacyPortfolio or Yo‐AI that are used for:

  • notifying all stakeholders of any communication issues
  • routing requests and tasks to my AI Agents who respond and act on my behalf
  • negotiating access rights to personal datasets
  • monitoring and reporting on compliance with data processing agreements

Above all, a TrustedSender is someone I want to engage with to seize opportunities while minimizing risk for both of us.
